# Delivery plan to CDA 2026

Planning date: 9 September 2026. Application closes 2 November; official later milestones are semi-final judging on 19 November, final judging on 1 December and ceremony on 11 December [S1]. Confirm any portal-specific time zone, cut-off hour and material-change rules; they are not established by the public page.

## Capacity

One human founder, with AI assistance during active development sessions. Plan approximately 18 focused human hours per week, adjustable after the first week. Human review, customer interviews and Khmer validation consume that time too. External test participants or an independent evidence reviewer are not a second developer. No automatic background operator or 24/7 service is assumed.

## Realistic evidence target

Start with 120 permissioned, deduplicated evaluation examples, balanced between suspicious and benign, plus an explicitly separate ambiguous set. Target 30 usability participants and two or more frontline staff for paired case-preparation tasks. These are planning targets, not achieved numbers. The previous 600-example aspiration is deferred until labelling capacity and independent review exist. A smaller well-labelled study is preferable to synthetic examples described as field evidence.

Keep examples from the same campaign/template together when splitting development/test sets. Use a blinded human review on a subset where possible; AI cannot independently validate its own labels. Record disagreements and show uncertainty intervals. A 30% reduction in median preparation time is a hypothesis to test, not a promise.

## Cut order when time is tight

Drop bot integration, maps, advanced graphs, native apps, live brand monitoring and elaborate AI summaries first. Keep privacy, unknown-on-failure, user correction, reviewer authorization and evaluation. If fewer than 12 human hours/week are available, restrict the entry to local message/link triage plus evidence intake and a transparent formative study. Do not ship a public service to meet a demo deadline.

## Weekly operating rhythm

Choose one user-visible outcome at the beginning of each working week. Implement small reviewed changes, run regression tests and show the actual build. End with an evidence-ledger update and scope decision. Keep completed, synthetic, planned and blocked statuses distinct. Changes after the application freeze should be stability fixes unless award rules permit an updated product submission.
