# Release gates: local prototype is not a public service

Do not accept real citizen evidence on a public endpoint until the following are implemented and reviewed:

- Named staff identity, MFA, tenant/case authorization and negative access tests.
- Documented data purpose, consent, external-processing permissions, correction/withdrawal and locally qualified legal review.
- Isolated bounded image worker, durable jobs, controlled provider calls, quotas and explicit partial/unknown outcomes.
- Provider licensing, key management, data minimization, no secrets in logs or URLs.
- Scheduled retention, deletion from observations and appropriate backup handling, tested restore and incident response.
- Fixed reviewed aggregate publication, disclosure review, no individual drill-down or implied national representativeness.
- Khmer review, narrow-device and accessibility tests, held-out performance evaluation with documented errors.
- Owner-approved deployment, account/budget decisions, written partner permissions and truthful public wording.

A local key is not production authentication. A passing test suite is not a compliance certification. Do not remove these gates to meet an award deadline. Drop optional scope first.
