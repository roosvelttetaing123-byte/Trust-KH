# CDA 2026 — public-safe competition brief

**Working project:** Trust.kh — Check before you trust.
**Application stage:** draft; no submission or award is claimed.

## Application narrative
Trust.kh is a Cambodian evidence-intake prototype designed to help people understand suspicious digital interactions and help authorized teams prepare better cases. A citizen can check a selected message or link, see explainable warning signs and analysis gaps, and separately choose to save a minimized report. An analyst reviews relevance; institutional readers receive bounded aggregate information rather than private conversations.

The research question is whether user-confirmed extraction, structured intake and transparent evidence improve case-preparation time and recommended-action comprehension compared with an existing manual workflow. The intended commercial model is free citizen checking supported by paid organizational workflow and deployment services; demand and willingness to pay remain to be validated.

Kapea already advertises identifier lookup and scam reporting, while Singapore's ScamShield supports screenshot checking. We do not claim to be the first scam checker or to replace official channels. The proposed advantage is the measured quality of the local intake workflow, not mere use of AI. [S2, S3]

## What a credible submission needs
A working demonstration; complete capability/limitation table; documented authorization for any pilot data; independently reviewed Khmer wording; a baseline comparison; error analysis; two genuine design-partner letters or equivalent evidence; realistic operating costs; ownership/category confirmation. Targets are not achieved metrics.
