# CDA 2026 submission checklist

Provisional category: Digital Research & Innovation, open to individuals and institutions on the official page [S1]. Minister's Startup is an alternative only after confirming applicant eligibility and registration requirements. Do not fabricate a second human team member or imply endorsement through employment.

Application deadline: 2 November 2026. Plan internal completion by 1 November. Semi-final 19 November; final 1 December; ceremony 11 December [S1]. Confirm the portal cutoff time and current requirements directly. No numerical judging weights have been established.

## Applicant verification

Confirm legal applicant name, citizenship/residency eligibility, project IP ownership, employer/outside-project permissions where applicable, category, any registration documents, working product name/domain and rights to supplied media. Keep identity documents and detailed business proposal private.

## Evidence package

Project profile; clear problem and solution; implemented/planned capability matrix; architecture and privacy map; working demonstration with labelled synthetic data; evaluation methodology and results with denominators; customer discovery evidence; actual partner letters where permission exists; operational plan and realistic business assumptions.

## Research contribution to validate

Measure whether structured user-corrected evidence intake and explicit uncertainty improve Khmer-speaking users' comprehension and reviewer case-preparation work. Use a baseline, deduplicated held-out cases, human labels and disclosed limitations. Existing screenshot checking elsewhere and Kapea's advertised capabilities must be acknowledged [S2,S3].

## Three-minute demonstration

0:00–0:30: fictional suspicious interaction and a user's decision.
0:30–1:10: warning reasons plus an unknown example; no safe guarantee.
1:10–1:45: separate consent and export; explain what is actually implemented.
1:45–2:20: reviewer relevance decision and qualified association.
2:20–2:45: limited aggregate view; no private messages.
2:45–3:00: measured outcomes, limitations and a concrete pilot request.

The detailed proposal is delivered privately to the founder. No submission has been made and no partner or result should be entered without evidence.
